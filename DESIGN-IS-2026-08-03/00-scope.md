# VAPOR UI/UX Redesign Scope

- Surface: `/` control center and `/demo/prava` judge journey.
- Primary user: US finance/FinOps leader controlling employee SaaS, cloud, and AI-agent spend.
- Primary task: understand a risky purchase, approve/block it, and verify the payment outcome with evidence.
- Constraints: Next.js 15, responsive web, hackathon demo clarity, WCAG AA, provider truth, no secret/card exposure.
- Reference: Frentix Fintech Mobile Wallet case study. Use its summary-first hierarchy and confident dark-fintech mood, not its protected brand, exact screens, palette, copy, or assets.
- Product authority: `plans/07-vapor-master-execution-system.md`, `docs/HACKATHON_SCOPE.md`, and `docs/EVIDENCE/CONTRACT_MANIFEST.md`.
